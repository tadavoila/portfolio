Trollfest

Ray Troll and the Ratfish Wranglers · Centennial Hall · Juneau, AK

Assistant Projection Designer

I assistant projection designed the concert "Trollfest" in Juneau which featured The Heists, Whiskey Class, and Ray Troll and the Ratfish Wranglers in their final concert in Centennial Hall (three interconnected ballrooms). I designed all auxillary media (wall projections, image maginifications (IMAG), and side tree projections), and I created projections for a third of the Ratfish Wranglers main set by animating Ray Troll's art. We mapped 4 7.5K laser projectors (set on scaffolding) and 1 hanging center projector to build the display for the main screen. I controlled two hanging projectors for wall projections and two hanging projectors for image magnification, for a total of nine projectors and 11 video feeds (double for the IMAG). I used Adobe After Effects, Adobe Photoshop, and QLab to design the projections.
