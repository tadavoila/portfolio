Projections and Video Design Portfolio

Moonlight Symphony

I projection designed "Moonlight Symphony," a Sailor Moon-inspired dance choreographed by Milana Beck. I used Adobe After Effects, p5.js, Three.js, and QLab to design the projections and implemented them using two stacked 10K projectors.

Indecent

I assistant projection designed the play Indecent by Paula Vogel which was directed by Sara Rademacher and projection designed by Elijah Frankle. I used Adobe After Effects and QLab to design the projections and implemented them with four 7.5K laser projectors — two on house left and two on house right.

Celestial Existence

I designed the short film "Celesital Existence" made of generative visuals surrounding the concept that progress arises from consistent imagination, transition, and evolution, and introspection is the heart of creativity. I used TouchDesigner and p5.js to design the media, and I used Blender to edit the film and the music.
