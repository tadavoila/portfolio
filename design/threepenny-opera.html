The Threepenny Opera

UCSB Division of Theater · Performing Arts Theater · Santa Barbara, CA

Projection Design

I projection designed the play The Threepenny Opera by Bertolt Brecht and directed by Annie Torsiglieri. I designed a live projection system where the actors would storm in, roll in a 7.5K laser projector on a cart, and film the scene with a camcorder. The video feed was displayed live as a newscast. I used Adobe After Effects, Adobe Photoshop, and QLab to design the projections.
