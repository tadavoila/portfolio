Daily Nexus Grades: Transparency in Academic Outcomes

UCSB · Daily Nexus Data Desk · Santa Barbara, CA · 2023–2024

Data Journalist / Visualization Engineer
I built and maintained the grade distribution reporting tools for the Daily Nexus, compiling multi‑year UCSB course outcomes and surfacing patterns in grading rigor, enrollment shifts, and departmental variance. These visuals informed two investigative articles and the public-facing grades explorer. The searchable Grades tool is linked below.
