Lighting Design Portfolio

By the Bog of Cats


I lighting designed the play By the Bog of Cats by Marina Carr. The lighting centers around the concept that envy and selfishness are fundamental human traits that can irreparably sever long-held relationships. I drew the plot and section with Vectorworks.

Chronology of Love

I lighting designed the song "Chronology of Love." The lighting centers around the concept that love is more than infatuation. Love requires time and effort to understand one another, and it can transcend beyond death.

Inopia

I lighting designed the song "Inopia" by Michael Stutz. The lighting centers around the concept that all human beings should be valued and appreciated.

Bus Stop

I lighting designed the play "Bus Stop" by William Inge. The lighting centers around the concept that accepting one's own flaws and vulnerabilities and actively working to acknowledge or change them paves the way for personal growth and genuine, meaningful connections with others.
