Fabrication Design Portfolio

3D-Printed Sandals

I designed a pair of 3D-printed sandals perfectly mapped to my feet. I used Fusion 360 to design the sandals, and after a rigorous testing process, I used a Bambu printer with TPU 95A to print the sandals. The shoes are soft, comfortable, and sturdy.

Flower Lampshades

I created two parametric flower lampshade designs for a small lightbulb with Rhino 7 and Grasshopper, sliced my design in Cura, and 3D printed my first shade on a Prusa i3 printer and my second shade on an UltiMaker S5 printer using PLA.
