Pattern Illustrator is an interactive web-based tool for creating complex geometric patterns through an intuitive visual interface. The tool enables users to explore mathematical relationships and generate intricate designs by combining basic shapes, transformations, and repetition patterns. Users can experiment with various parameters to create unique visual compositions, export their creations, and share their work with others.

The application provides real-time feedback as users adjust parameters, making it easy to understand how different mathematical operations affect the final design. Whether for artistic exploration, educational purposes, or design projects, Pattern Illustrator offers a flexible platform for creative expression through computational geometry.
