Afterlight: A Waltz Through Time

UCSB Division of Dance · Hatlen Theater · Santa Barbara, CA · 2025

Lighting Designer

I lighting designed "A Waltz Through Time", a Howl's Moving Castle-inspired dance choreographed by Milana Beck for the Afterlight concert directed by Christina McCarthy. I programmed the lighting using the ETC Eos console.
